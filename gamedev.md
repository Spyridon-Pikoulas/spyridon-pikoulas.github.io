# HeyHeyThere game assets

HeyHeyThere is me, Spyridon Pikoulas, a software developer in Greece. I make game assets for
Unity, Godot and any other engine, and look after every pack and its support myself.

## What the packs cover

- **2D effects:** sprite sheets of spells, hits, explosions, buffs and weather, as animated
  prefabs in Unity and as scenes in Godot.
- **Interface kits:** pixel-art UI in three themes (classic, sci-fi and fantasy), 9-sliced, with
  icons and a matching font, and 200 icons in the same style for items, gear, stats and menus.
- **Pixel fonts:** bitmap-style fonts for games, as TextMesh Pro assets in Unity.
- **Sound effects:** 8-bit game sounds and soft interface sounds, as WAV and OGG.
- **Shaders:** a PS1 look, screen effects and scene transitions for Godot.
- **PS1-style 3D:** low-poly props, weapons, level kits and textures.

## How they are made

Every pack is made in code: the effects are rendered by shaders, the pixel art and fonts are set
pixel by pixel, the sounds are synthesised, and the 3D models and textures are built by code run
in Blender. No generative image, audio or 3D model, and no samples or recordings, go into them.
The code is written with the help of AI (Anthropic's Claude), and each pack is checked by
automated tests before it ships.
